# Bank Transfer Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how bank-transfer questions should
be handled.

## Investigation Steps

When a customer asks about a bank transfer:

1. Identify the customer.
2. Retrieve the relevant transaction.
3. Check the transaction amount.
4. Check the currency.
5. Check the transaction status.

## Customer Response

The agent may explain the current transaction status and
available transaction information.

The agent must not invent information that is not available
in the system.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The transaction cannot be identified.
- The customer disputes the transaction.
- The requested action requires human authorisation.

## Restrictions

The AI agent must not:

- Create a bank transfer.
- Modify transaction records.
- Claim that a transfer succeeded without evidence.
