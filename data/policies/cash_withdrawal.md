# Cash Withdrawal Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how cash withdrawal questions should
be investigated.

## Investigation Steps

When a customer asks about a cash withdrawal:

1. Identify the customer.
2. Retrieve the withdrawal transaction.
3. Check the amount.
4. Check the currency.
5. Check the location.
6. Check the transaction status.

## Customer Response

The agent may provide the transaction information available
in the system.

If the customer disputes the withdrawal, the case should
be reviewed further.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The withdrawal cannot be identified.
- The customer disputes the transaction.
- Additional investigation is required.

## Restrictions

The AI agent must not:

- Modify transaction records.
- Claim that a withdrawal was reversed without evidence.
- Access another customer's transaction history.
