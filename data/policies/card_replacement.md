# Card Replacement Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how card-replacement requests should
be handled.

## Customer Response

The agent may provide general information about the card
replacement process.

The agent should first verify the customer's identity using
the available customer information before accessing
customer-specific information.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- Identity cannot be verified.
- The requested action requires human authorisation.
- The customer reports an issue that cannot be resolved
  using the available information.

## Restrictions

The AI agent must not:

- Reveal another customer's information.
- Modify customer records without authorisation.
- Issue or activate a replacement card.
