# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how refund requests should be handled.

## Investigation Steps

When a customer requests a refund:

1. Identify the customer.
2. Retrieve the relevant transaction.
3. Verify the merchant, amount, currency and transaction
   status.
4. Check whether the transaction is eligible for further
   review.
5. Provide the customer with the next appropriate step.

## Customer Response

The AI agent may explain:

- The transaction details.
- Whether the transaction was completed, failed or refunded.
- Whether additional review is required.

The AI agent must not state that a refund has been approved
unless an authorised system explicitly confirms this.

## Escalation

Escalate when:

- The transaction cannot be identified.
- The customer disputes the transaction information.
- The requested action requires an authorised human decision.
- The available information is insufficient.

## Restrictions

The AI agent must not:

- Automatically approve a refund.
- Modify transaction records.
- Promise a refund.
- Claim that a refund has been processed without evidence.
